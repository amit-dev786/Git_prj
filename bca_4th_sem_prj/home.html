COMPUTER'S HARDWARE

What is Hardware?

Hardware refers to the physical components that humans use to build useful systems and devices. In computing, hardware helps perform tasks faster, smoother, and more efficiently.

Here, we discuss computer and networking hardware devices that power the digital world and connect people globally.

Computer & Networking

Hardware and networking form the backbone of modern computer systems. Hardware includes physical devices, while networking connects systems to share data and resources.

Today, businesses, schools, hospitals, and organizations rely heavily on hardware and networking for smooth operations.
